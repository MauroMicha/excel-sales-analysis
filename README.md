# Sales Performance Analysis — Excel Project

## Overview
Analysis of a retail sales dataset to identify top-performing product categories 
and seasonal sales trends, using Excel Pivot Tables and Dynamic Charts. 

**Tools used:** Microsoft Excel (Power Query, Pivot Tables, Dynamic Charts)  
**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset — Kaggle  
**Level:** Beginner — first data analysis project  

## Business Question
*Which product categories generate the most revenue, and in which months do 
sales peak?*
---
## Key Findings
1. **Furniture is the top revenue-generating category**, accounting for nearly 
49% of total sales. Technology follows at 29%, while Office Supplies represents 
the remaining 22%.

2. **Sales peak in November and September**, likely driven by end-of-year 
corporate purchasing cycles. February is consistently the weakest month, 
generating less than 2% of annual sales.

3. **The November peak is driven primarily by Furniture**, which contributes 
over $92M in that month alone. Technology shows a more stable demand pattern 
throughout the year.
